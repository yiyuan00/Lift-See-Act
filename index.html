Why LSA?

Learn more than a sparse trajectory

  • Human videos are abundant, but they do not provide robot actions or explicit 3D structure.
  • Existing pipelines keep sparse hand trajectories and discard rich intermediate knowledge from 3D foundation models.
  • LSA transfers dense geometry, interaction focus, and 3D motion into one reusable visual policy encoder.

Main pipeline

One hierarchy, three stages

  • The data engine lifts diverse human videos into reusable 3D supervision.
  • Hierarchical pretraining injects knowledge at the layer where it is most useful.
  • The frozen encoder then supports lightweight closed-loop robot control.
Novel contribution highlighted by reviewers

See phase

Dual guidance makes 3D knowledge actionable

  • Attentive guidance transfers the model's own strongest hand-object attention pattern to shallow layers.
  • Structural guidance reconstructs metric depth, turning implicit 3D features into explicit spatial understanding.
1

Build a soft hand prior

\[\begin{aligned}q_k(x,y)&=\frac{(x-x_k)^2+(y-y_k)^2}{2\sigma^2}\\ H(x,y)&=\max_k\exp(-q_k)\\ h&=\operatorname{Softmax}(H/\tau)\end{aligned}\]

The 21 detected hand keypoints form a smooth spatial cue at patch resolution.

2

Score the model's emerging attention

\[s_{n,k}=\frac{1}{N_a}\sum_{i=1}^{N_a}a_{n,k,i}\,h_i\]

Each shallow head is measured by how well its CLS-to-patch attention overlaps the hand prior.

3

Select—not prescribe—the target

\[(n^*,k^*)=\arg\max_{n,k}s_{n,k},\qquad a^*=a_{n^*,k^*}\]

The hand cue chooses the best internally emerged attention map rather than replacing attention with a heatmap.

4

Self-distill across shallow layers

\[\begin{aligned}\tilde a^*&=\phi(\operatorname{sg}(a^*)),\quad \tilde a_n=\phi(\bar a_n)\\ \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{att}}&=\sum_{n=1}^{R}\left\|\tilde a^*-\tilde a_n\right\|_2^2\end{aligned}\]

Here, \(\bar a_n\) averages attention heads, \(\phi\) applies Min-Max normalization, and \(\operatorname{sg}\) stops gradients. Guidance covers the first \(R=6\) layers after a 10-epoch motion-planning warm-up.

5

Add explicit structural guidance

\[\begin{aligned}\hat D&=\operatorname{Dec}(X_m)\\ \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{recon}}&=\left\|\hat D-D^g\right\|_2^2\end{aligned}\]

A transformer decoder reconstructs metric depth from intermediate policy tokens, constraining the encoder to retain scene structure.

Insight
  • The hand prior is only a selector. The supervision target comes from the model itself, so it can retain object context and head diversity.
  • Motion creates interaction awareness. Predicting future hand motion couples the hand with the manipulated object, allowing attention to extend beyond the hand and transfer to robot scenes.
